Working conclusion

Two learned configurations now frame the result. CNN first-order AR reaches 5.06 / 9.55 WER at 11.7 Hz. The local-history Transformer AR with BiGRU residual pooling—seeds 3407, 3408, and 3409—reaches 4.89 / 9.43 at 10.3 Hz. All three paired seeds improve on clean and other, though test-other remains variable. Order-aware pooling is therefore promising, with the clean-speech result providing the stronger evidence.
