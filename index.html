1 · The collapse, and the fix

Teacher-forced NLL from an undertrained decoder plus a per-frame entropy bonus drove the boundary probabilities to a uniform mush: the argmax produced ~0 boundaries, the decoder saw ~1 token, WER hit 100%.

Diagnosis. Entropy pushed every p→0.5 (the rate loss constrains only the sum, not sharpness) → uniform p, empty argmax. And teacher-forced NLL under-rewards audio → RL pushed ρ down. Fix: drop the entropy bonus + warm the decoder longer (2→6 epochs).
