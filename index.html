Working conclusion

Two learned configurations now frame the result. CNN first-order AR reaches 5.06 / 9.55 WER at a 0.234 kept ratio. Full-prefix Transformer AR with BiGRU residual pooling reaches 4.92 / 9.66 at 0.215. BiGRU improves test-clean for all three paired seeds relative to mean pooling, but test-other is mixed and more variable. Order-aware pooling is therefore promising for clean speech; robustness on harder speech remains unresolved.
